About me

My name is Nina Thompson and I am a professional hairstylist with over 10 years of experience. I specialize in creating unique hairstyles that help my clients express themselves. I am located in the heart of Dublin city and am very passionate about my work. I love the look on my clients' faces when they see their hair in ways they have never before. I specialize in all type of hair, but I have a special love for braids and anything 4C hair. I am also a firm believer in good products for the hair, and I only want the best products for all my clients.
